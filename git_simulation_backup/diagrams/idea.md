# Project Title:
FleetOS – A Unified Fleet Operations Management System

---

## 1. Project Overview

FleetOS is a web-based Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) application designed to manage and monitor commercial transportation fleets. The system integrates vehicle management, driver management, trip tracking, route planning, and financial operations into a single platform.

The application is intended for logistics companies, bus operators, and enterprise fleet owners who require centralized operational control over their transportation assets.

---

## 2. Objective of the Project

The primary objective of FleetOS is to develop a scalable and modular fleet management system that enables:

- Real-time vehicle monitoring
- Efficient trip and dispatch management
- Driver performance tracking
- Route and schedule planning
- Operational reporting and analytics
- Financial tracking related to fleet operations

The system aims to provide a unified dashboard that simplifies fleet administration and improves operational visibility.

---

## 3. Scope of the Project

FleetOS covers the following functional areas:

### 3.1 Vehicle Management
- Registration of vehicles
- Maintenance of vehicle profiles (VIN, model, insurance details)
- Status tracking (Active, Idle, Maintenance)

### 3.2 Driver Management
- Driver profile creation
- License information management
- Performance monitoring
- Trip assignment

### 3.3 Trip & Dispatch Management
- Creation and assignment of trips
- Start and end trip tracking
- Trip history records
- Electronic Proof of Delivery (ePOD)

### 3.4 Route & Transit Management
- Route creation and stop management
- Schedule configuration
- Live Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA) tracking
- Passenger-facing tracking interface (for bus systems)

### 3.5 Monitoring & Alerts
- Real-time vehicle location tracking
- Overspeed detection
- Idle time monitoring
- Custom rule-based alerts

### 3.6 Financial Management
- Trip revenue recording
- Expense tracking
- Driver wallet management
- Maintenance cost records

### 3.7 Reporting & Analytics
- Distance traveled reports
- Fuel consumption insights
- Trip summaries
- Operational performance metrics

---

## 4. System Users

The system supports multiple user roles:

- Admin / Fleet Manager
- Dispatcher
- Route Planner
- Driver
- Passenger (Transit Module)
- Finance Manager

Each role has role-based access to relevant features.

---

## 5. Technology Stack

### Backend
- Node.js with Express.js
- TypeScript

### Frontend
- Next.js (React Framework)
- Tailwind CSS

### Database
- MongoDB or PostgreSQL

The system follows a modular architecture with RESTful API communication between frontend and backend.

---

## 6. Expected Outcome

Upon completion, FleetOS will function as a full-stack fleet operations management platform capable of:

- Managing fleet data efficiently
- Tracking vehicles and trips in real time
- Generating operational reports
- Supporting multi-role user access
- Handling scalable data storage

---

## 7. Future Enhancements

- AI-based route optimization
- Predictive maintenance alerts

## Conclusion

FleetOS is designed as a comprehensive fleet operations management system that centralizes transportation management into a single digital platform. The system integrates vehicle monitoring, trip management, route planning, and financial tracking to provide structured and organized fleet control.
